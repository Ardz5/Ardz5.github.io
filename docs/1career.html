Reflection on Career Aspirations 

My chosen career path as mentioned previously in the home page is to work in Quantum Computing specifically in the aspect of quantum computer architecture and contribute to the development of quantum Systems. The different workshops and guest lectures are what inpired me to take initiative and find out my desired career.

Required Technial skills for Quantum Computing 

Quantum Mechanics & Quantum Theory - Knowledge and understanding of fundamental quantumn concepts such as superposition, entanglement and interference.

Mathematics Linear Algebra, Probability theory, Complex Numbers - Knowledge and understanding of these strand's of mathematics are essential. Matrices are powerful as they can be used to represent quantum gates. Probability theory is important becasue Quantum mechanics has a probabilistic nature and quantum computing relies heavily on probability to determine outcomes of quantum operations. Lastly understanding how to use complex numbers in this context is essential as it allows you to understand wavefunctions and quantum states.

Programming skills - Proficiency in a quantum programming language is required such as Qiskit (For IBM Quantumn Computers), Cirq (Python-based for Google’s quantum computers) or Quipper (For Microsoft Quantum Computers). Additional languages include classical languages such as C++, Python and Java, they are often used to implement hybrid quantum-classical algorithms or interface with quantum hardware.

Quantum Computing theory & Classical Computing - Being able to build quantum circuits and understand how quantum gates work. Deep knowledge of classical computing hardware and architecture is important as this is what quantum systems are based on to begin with, classical logic gates, become quantum gates.

Required Professional skills for Quantum Computing 

Critical thinking and flexability in problem-solving - Quantum computing requires a strong ability to think analytically and abstractly. Problems are often badly defined or not fully understood. Therefore it is important being comfortable with uncertainty and creative in problem-solving it is key when contributing to the field.

Collaboration and communication - Being able to work in teams and explain complex quantum concepts to both experts and non-experts is key.

Research skills - Quantum computing is a highly research-driven field, it's important being comfortable with reading research papers and contributing to new findings.
